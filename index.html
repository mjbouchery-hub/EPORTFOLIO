Hi, I'm Matthew.

I'm a developer, problem solver, and systems thinker.

I build practical software by understanding the systems behind the problem. My background in manufacturing, networking, and troubleshooting taught me that great solutions don't start with code—they start with understanding how everything works together.

My Approach

Understanding the problem before building the solution.

Understand First

I believe the best solutions begin with asking the right questions. Before I build anything, I want to understand how the system works, who will use it, and what problem we are actually trying to solve.

Build with Purpose

Once I understand the problem, I focus on creating practical, dependable software that is intuitive to use and built with the people using it in mind.

Keep Improving

Every project is an opportunity to learn. I enjoy refining my skills, improving my process, and finding better ways to build solutions that are genuinely useful.
